# VEIL demo film: three storylines

The one thing to remember: **a strategy game where the map is hidden from everyone, even its makers, and you can prove it when the war ends.**
The spine: one real match on veil.mrfomolabs.xyz, from invite to The Reveal. Every screen is captured from the live site; no mock UI.
Format: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 90–120 s. Music and sound effects, no voiceover (titles and the game's own text carry the words).

## A. The map nobody has (recommended)

**Device:** the result first. **Form:** one continuous take that dives into the board and comes back out.
The film opens on the ending: the fog burning off a whole archipelago and "✔ Verified: all 6 turns re-run" landing. Then it rewinds into the same war while it was still hidden, and shows why nobody, the builders included, could have seen or bent it.

- **First two seconds:** clouds tear open across a finished war, seat colours everywhere. The verified line stamps in gold.
- **Beats:** hook (The Reveal, verified) → tension ("every strategy game server sees the whole map") → reveal (VEIL title rises out of the fog) → proof (invite link → seal orders 🔒 → the sealed machine rolls the dice → buy intel from a warlord) → payoff (back to the fog lifting, now earned; scrub through the hidden orders) → claim ("Hidden while you play. Proven when it ends.") → coda (winnings sealed into the fog: Gloam private cash-out) → end card (VEIL mark, veil.mrfomolabs.xyz).
- **Look:** the game's dusk purple and gold, Cinzel titles, real screens at 1.6–2×; the fog itself is the transition between scenes.
- **Sound:** low wind bed; a wax-seal thud on each seal; a deep drum on battles; a rising shimmer on the reveal (the game's own cues, recorded from the site, plus a music bed).

## B. Game night

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain.
Two friends, one invite link. The film follows a real two-player match: the link becomes the invite card, the card becomes the lobby, the lobby becomes the islands. A truce, a betrayal behind a cloud, and The Reveal shows what each of them really did.

- **First two seconds:** the game's own invite screen: "You're invited to a war · hosted by Storm Crane", the cursor already on "Join the war".
- **Beats:** hook (the invite) → tension (two war names, one fogged board, neither sees the other) → reveal (VEIL title) → proof (march, seal, the 🔒 marks appear next to each name, the recap: "Won 1 battle") → payoff (The Reveal: the hidden army behind the cloud, verified) → claim ("Play with friends. Trust no one. Check everything.") → end card.
- **Look:** warmer, more people-first: the war names and the seal marks carry the drama; close punch-ins on the top bar and the recap card.
- **Sound:** a lighter, rhythmic bed; the seal marks tick in like notifications; a single gong when a capital falls.

## C. Inside the cloud (the bold one)

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** an endless zoom.
The camera dives into one cloud over an island and keeps going: the cloud is ciphertext, the ciphertext opens only inside the sealed machine where the dice roll, the dice's proof is checked by the chain, and the zoom finally comes out the far side into The Reveal, where every layer is plain.

- **First two seconds:** a single cloud over a fogged island fills the frame; the camera is already moving into it.
- **Beats:** hook (dive into the cloud) → tension (the cloud is bytes nobody can read) → reveal (the sealed machine: VEIL title inside it) → proof (orders open, the VRF dice roll, the shield's proof checks on chain, a warlord sells what it sees) → payoff (out into The Reveal, verified) → claim → coda (coins sink into the fog: Gloam) → end card.
- **Look:** each layer its own world: the painted islands, raw hex on near-black, the gold proof, then back to daylight over the archipelago.
- **Sound:** wind that thins into a low electronic hum inside the machine, a clean click per layer, the shimmer on the way out.
